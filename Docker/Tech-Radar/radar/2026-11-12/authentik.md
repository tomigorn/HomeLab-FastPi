The single login (Holy Grail) for every web UI, with invite-only sign-up and mandatory MFA.
Identity provider over OIDC/OAuth2 for apps with their own login (Nextcloud, Jellyfin, Grafana, Home Assistant, Audiobookshelf, the VPN UI and more); forward auth for tools that have no login of their own.
Branded, with a dashboard that greys out LAN-only apps.
