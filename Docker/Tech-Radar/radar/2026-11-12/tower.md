Unraid 7.3 NAS on a Celeron N5105 with 16 GB RAM, a btrfs RAID1 pool of two SSDs and a 6 TB HDD.
Runs Nextcloud; its whole setup lives in a repo as configuration-as-code.
Sleeps when idle and wakes by Wake-on-LAN on the first request. Under a hardware burn-in test until about 2026-12-01 (unexplained power-offs), array stopped meanwhile.
