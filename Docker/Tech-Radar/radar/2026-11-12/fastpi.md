Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB RAM, a 512 GB NVMe drive and two 2 TB USB disks, running Debian 13; always on.
Runs the edge (Cloudflare Tunnel, Traefik, CrowdSec, Authentik), DNS, the VPN, monitoring, the audiobook stack and the wakers.
More than 60 containers on one small board.
