Media and compute server: Ubuntu, Intel i5-11400 with Quick Sync transcoding, 61 GB RAM.
Holds a 1 TB NVMe plus a mergerfs pool of SSDs and a 28 TB HDD that spins down.
Sleeps when idle; fastpi wakes it with Wake-on-LAN in about 45 s.
