Media and compute server: Ubuntu 26.04, Intel i5-11400 with Quick Sync transcoding, 64 GB RAM.
Holds a 1 TB NVMe plus a mergerfs pool of two 8 TB SSDs and a 30 TB HDD that spins down.
Sleeps when idle; fastpi wakes it with Wake-on-LAN in about 45 s.
