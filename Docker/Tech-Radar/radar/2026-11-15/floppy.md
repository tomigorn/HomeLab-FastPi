Per-user tracker for films, series, books and games: ratings, history and want-lists.
Fed by Jellyfin and Seerr webhooks; replaced Ryot on 2026-10-26.
Locked down at the edge after a security review: risky endpoints are blocked and the app may only reach the internet on web ports.
