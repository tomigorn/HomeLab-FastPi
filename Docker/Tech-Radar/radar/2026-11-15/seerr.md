Request page for films and series on beefy: friends pick a title, Radarr or Sonarr fetch it, Jellyfin shows when it is ready.
Runs an own image with the not-yet-released OIDC login, rebuilt weekly.
Own small scripts injected into the page add an indexer usage bar, a "download now" button and labels for titles that wait for the night.
