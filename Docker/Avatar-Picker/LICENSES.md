# Third-party assets

No AI-generated images are used anywhere in the gallery. All images are built by
`scripts/build-gallery.py` (sources and crop boxes documented there).

## Rows 1-2: public-domain artwork (Wikimedia Commons)

Every file below was fetched through the Commons API; its file page carries a
public-domain licence tag (checked 2026-10-18) and the artist died before 1956,
so the work is public domain in Switzerland (author's life + 70 years) and the
US. Original period artwork only (no modern redraws or fan art). Images were
cropped to a square on the face/subject and resized to 512x512.
