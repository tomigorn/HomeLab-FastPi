# Third-party assets

No AI-generated images are used anywhere in the gallery. All images are built by
`scripts/build-gallery.py` (sources and crop boxes documented there).

## Public-domain artwork (Wikimedia Commons)

Rows: Classic storybook, Art classics, Japanese woodblock, Belle Époque posters,
Fairy-tale illustration, Early comics, Swiss masters.

Every file below was fetched through the Commons API; its extmetadata licence is
"Public domain", its file page carries the public-domain licence tag listed
(checked 2026-10-18) and the artist died before 1956, so the work is public
domain in Switzerland (author's life + 70 years) and the US. Original period
artwork only (no modern redraws or fan art). Images were cropped to a square on
the face/subject and resized to 512x512.

## Local pack: pop-culture characters (not in this repository)

Rows: Disney & Pixar (27), Animated films (27), Movies (27), Lord of the Rings (27), Harry Potter (18), Marvel & DC (27), Cartoons (36), Vox Machina (9), TV series (36), Muppets & Sesame Street (18), Anime (63), Pokémon (18), Video games (36), Retro games (9), Super Mario (18), LEGO (18), Dinosaurs (18), Memes (18); freely licensed and public-domain rows kept here too: Animals (27), Space (27), Mythology (18), Historic figures (18), Pirates & knights (9).

These images are **third-party copyrighted character art, film/TV stills and
posters** (and, for the meme animals, third-party photographs), owned by their
respective studios, artists and photographers. They are used privately in a
LAN-only, login-protected homelab avatar gallery for a handful of users, are
**not redistributed**, and are **not part of this repository**:
`scripts/build-gallery.py` writes them to `app/img-local/`, which is gitignored
and only baked into the private Docker image. `gallery.json` lists their names
and ids (`"pack": "local"`); a fresh clone simply hides them. Each image was
cropped to the character's head/face and resized to 512x512 (transparent areas
filled with the avatar colour; crop boxes never run past an opaque source, see
`tests/test_gallery.py`, `test_no_edge_band`).

Sources (v1.5.2: every crop box lies inside its source image, no padding; a
transparent source is laid on the avatar colour like an emoji and flagged
`"square": true` in `gallery.json`): the studios' official character portraits
(Disney: frozen.disney.com, princess.disney.com, characters.disney.com,
cars.disney.com, movies.disney.com; Illumination: illumination.com; DreamWorks:
dreamworks.com; Aardman: aardman.com), the character's (or film's) Wikipedia
infobox image (English or another language edition; low-resolution non-free
file; the Lord of the Rings row uses film stills from the characters' English and Italian articles: character art, official posters, promotional or in-character film/TV
stills), Wikimedia Commons (meme animals, Pac-Man,
SpongeBob, Patrick, Finn, Jake, the Creeper, the Pac-Man ghosts; public-domain
studio publicity stills and screenshots - Mary Poppins, Dorothy (1939), King Kong
(1933), Godzilla (1954), Lassie, Spock (1967); and freely licensed or public-domain
photographs of character figures, props and puppets - the Minion statue, the
R2-D2 and C-3PO props, the E.T. and Katniss figures at Madame Tussauds, the Toothless
statue, the TARDIS and Dalek props, the Kermit, Elmo, Big Bird and Oscar puppets;
the photos are
free, the depicted characters remain copyrighted), and Studio Ghibli's official
stills, which the studio offers for free use "within the bounds of common sense"
(https://www.ghibli.jp/info/013344/).

v1.9 sources: Harry Potter, Marvel & DC, the new Muppets and the Movies / TV series refills are infobox images of the characters' English or Italian Wikipedia articles plus Commons photos of props, puppets, exhibits and wax figures (Dobby, Hedwig, the Batman suit, the Captain America figure at Madame Tussauds, a Hulk statue, a Cookie Monster vending-machine decal, Rosita, Mr. Bean at the Polonia Wax Museum, K9, KITT, the Weeping Angel, Bert, Statler and Waldorf, Gonzo, the Swedish Chef) and public-domain publicity photos (James Bond 1964, Flipper); a Cyberman costume photo; Hermione, Spider-Man, Iron Man, Black Widow, Groot and the ten Muppets moved rows and keep their images.
