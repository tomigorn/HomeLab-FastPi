# Skip intro and credits

While you watch, a **Skip intro** or **Skip credits** button appears. Nothing is ever skipped by itself: you press it, or you don't.

| Works in | Skip intro / credits |
|---|---|
| Browser, phone app, Samsung TV app | yes, out of the box |

## Recaps and previews
"Previously on…" and previews have no button by default. Turn it on **once per device**:

1. **Settings → Playback → Advanced** (at the bottom).
2. Set **Ask to skip** for recap and preview.

## New episodes
The buttons are prepared at night. A new episode gets its buttons **the next day**; until then it plays normally without them.
