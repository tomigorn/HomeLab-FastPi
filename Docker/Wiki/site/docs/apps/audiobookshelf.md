# Listen to audiobooks

**Address:** `https://audiobookshelf.holy-grail.ch`

Audiobooks are always ready. This server never sleeps, so there is no waiting.

## In the browser (first login)
1. Open `https://audiobookshelf.holy-grail.ch`.
2. Press **Login with Holy Grail**.
3. Log in with your username, password and authenticator code.
4. You see the libraries. Pick a book.
5. Press the **play** button.

Your place is saved to your account. Next time you carry on where you stopped.

## On your phone (app)
1. Install the **Audiobookshelf** app (App Store or Google Play).
2. Open it. As server address type `https://audiobookshelf.holy-grail.ch`.
3. Press **Submit**.
4. Press the button to log in with OpenID (**Login with OpenID**).
5. Log in with Holy Grail.
6. Pick a book and press **play**.

Tip: download a book in the app to listen without internet.

## A book is missing
[Request it](audiobook-requests.md). It appears here after the download, normally within minutes to hours.
